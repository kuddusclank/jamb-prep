# JAMB syllabus research and proposed topic catalogue

Status: researched planning inventory; not seeded or claimed as an exhaustive leaf-level curriculum. Retrieved 11 October 2026 from the official [IBASS e-syllabus](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/e-syllabus). All 25 linked PDFs were downloaded and text-inspected. The portal does not establish a single current-exam-year version for every PDF. Full numbered contents/objectives, prescribed passages/texts and specialist language labels must be reconciled before implementation acceptance. The paraphrased groups below cover each published subject and organise the admin taxonomy; they do not replace the official documents.

Evidence: public portal bundle `main.221b9987.js` references these documents. Source identifiers, hashes and page counts are retained in `jamb-syllabus-sources.json`. Full PDF text is intentionally not copied into this repository. Subject availability in an examination must be checked separately from presence in the syllabus portal.

## 1. Agriculture science

Agricultural foundations: scope, importance, ecology, inheritance, production inputs, Nigerian agricultural development, public and nongovernmental support. Crop production: soil origins, water and conservation, fertility, cultivation, plant structures, development and reproduction, propagation, planting systems, crop management, forage, ornamental plants, weeds, diseases, pests and forestry. Animals: classification, husbandry terminology, anatomy, reproduction, feeding, management, disease prevention, fisheries, wildlife and beekeeping. Farm economics: production resources, economic principles and extension. Technology: surveying, equipment, mechanisation, processing/storage, biotechnology, digital applications and agricultural research.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/AgricPdf.9465f3cd595e9542868a.pdf) — 16 pages.

## 2. Arabic

Reading comprehension; translation between Arabic and English; written composition; literary study across historical periods. Grammar coverage: demonstratives, relatives, questions and conditions; gender and noun properties; dual and plural forms; possessive constructions; independent/attached pronouns; agreement and dependent constructions; prepositions; transitive/intransitive verbs; past, present and imperative forms; grammatical modifiers; basic and derived verb patterns; verbal nouns; adjectival constructions; conditional clauses; numbers; active/passive constructions; nominal sentences; accusative noun uses. Arabic-script labels and prescribed literary works require specialist editorial review.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Arabic.233fe7117fc534609a6e.pdf) — 5 pages.

## 3. Art

Classification and branches of art; visual elements and design principles; art vocabulary; historical development; African traditions; Nigerian historical traditions and crafts; modern Nigerian artists and practice; drawing, painting, sculpture and other production methods; equipment/material handling and care; interpretation and appreciation; social purposes of art; creative enterprise.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Artpdf.85197ba4c495458e1ba8.pdf) — 5 pages.

## 4. Biology

Organism characteristics and classification; diversity across major groups; structural, behavioural and functional adaptations. Life functions: flowering-plant structure, feeding, transport, respiration, excretion, support/movement, reproduction, development and coordination. Ecology: distribution factors, organism interactions, habitats, Nigerian biomes, population ecology, soils and human environmental effects. Variation, inheritance and biotechnology. Evolutionary explanations and supporting evidence.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Biology.0075e70afd357d7a39ae.pdf) — 12 pages.

## 5. Chemistry

Mixture separation and substance purification; chemical combination and quantitative relationships; particle behaviour and gases; atomic models, periodic relationships and bonding; nuclear processes; dissolution and solubility; environmental contamination; acids/alkalis/salts; oxidation/reduction; electrochemical processes; reaction heat and energy; reaction speed; reversible reactions and equilibrium; nonmetal elements and compounds; metals and their compounds; carbon chemistry and organic families; industrial chemistry; chemistry of astronomical environments. Include calculations, laboratory interpretation and safe practical reasoning as subtopic skills.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Chemistry.9efd0b98043d56922aaa.pdf) — 16 pages.

## 6. Christian religious studies

Early biblical themes: divine sovereignty, covenants, leadership, providence, parenting, obedience, David and decision making. Kingdom/exile themes: greed, divine authority, reforms, concern for Judah, faith/courage, Jonah, justice and authentic worship, holiness/calling, judgement and hope. Gospels/Acts: Jesus’ birth, baptism/temptation, discipleship, miracles, parables, mountain teaching, missions, Peter’s confession, transfiguration, entry into Jerusalem/temple cleansing, final meal, trials/death, resurrection/ascension, Jesus’ self-teaching, love, early fellowship, Spirit/mission, opposition and Gentile outreach. Epistles: justification, law/grace, renewed life, joint inheritance, humility, forgiveness, spiritual gifts, giving, citizenship, labour, second coming, impartiality, prayer, community conduct, corruption and sexual ethics.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/CRS.7f96f8e535ca408ca474.pdf) — 12 pages.

## 7. Commerce

Commerce and its role; occupations; production; domestic/foreign trade; buying and selling procedures; services supporting trade, including transport, communication, banking, insurance and warehousing; business forms; business funding; trade organisations; money; securities markets; management; marketing; commercial law; commodity markets; business environment and social responsibility.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Commerce.819d9c6929bc849d1cd9.pdf) — 8 pages.

## 8. Computer studies

Computing history and device classification; system fundamentals and information/data; input/output, memory and processing components; software categories and operating systems. Application packages: word processing, spreadsheets, databases, graphics, presentations and web design. Files and file operations; startup/shutdown, maintenance and room safety. ICT: communication systems, applications, internet, email, networking, web services, cables/connectors. Problem solving: programming language categories, algorithms/flowcharts, language structures, program development and system lifecycle. AI concepts, branches/applications and robotics foundations. Computing ethics, information security, cyber risk prevention, study opportunities and careers. The portal PDF introduction and detailed section organisation differ: map the detailed table rather than only its summary.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Computer.ea1ead8cea82f53d3717.pdf) — 23 pages.

## 9. Economics

Economic reasoning and scarcity; economic systems; analytical methods and quantitative tools; demand; consumer choice; supply; price formation; production; costs/revenue; market competition and imperfect markets; national output/income; money and inflation; financial intermediaries; public finance; growth/development; Nigerian agriculture; industry and industrialisation; resources in Nigeria’s economy; business organisation; population; international trade; international economic bodies; production factors and their remuneration.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Economics.da2a30ae8659e29c8bb4.pdf) — 9 pages.

## 10. French

Reading comprehension; French word classes and written sentence structure; grammatical transformations, vocabulary, meaning, idioms and proverbs; discrimination of speech sounds, liaison and related oral features; Francophone African and French culture/civilisation, including education, greetings, food, dress and social institutions. Preserve French accents in labels and content.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/French.170eeaabf96471e303c5.pdf) — 5 pages.

## 11. Geography

Geographic methods: maps, scale and distance/area calculations, bearings/gradients, relief profiles, statistical diagrams, elementary surveying and GIS. Physical geography: earth/solar relationships, coordinates/time, earth structure, rocks, tectonics and landforms, volcanism/seismic events, weathering/erosion/mass movement/deposition, oceans/lakes/rivers, atmospheric conditions, climate, vegetation, soils and environmental resources/issues. Human geography: populations, settlement, farming, fishing, forestry, mining, manufacturing, transport and trade. Regional geography: Nigeria’s regions, resources and human/economic systems; Africa and ECOWAS. Check the source’s lettered table rows individually during curriculum mapping.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Geography.0bf33e33f48e4139be13.pdf) — 9 pages.

## 12. Government

Government concepts and authority; governmental forms, branches, structures and systems; ideologies; constitutions; democratic principles; lawmaking; citizenship; voting/elections; parties; pressure groups; public opinion; public service. Nigeria: precolonial systems, colonial expansion, nationalist change, constitutional development before/after independence, institutions and commissions, party politics, federalism, public enterprises, local government and military intervention. International: foreign-policy principles, Nigerian external relations and African relationships; Nigeria’s membership and the organisation/functions/problems of international and regional bodies.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Government.92e42612590a98eeba28.pdf) — 8 pages.

## 13. Hausa

Language: orthography, reading comprehension, composition, translation, sound system, word formation, grammar and meaning. Literature: oral and written genres, literary techniques and prescribed texts. Culture: customary institutions, beliefs, family/life events, occupations, crafts, social practices and material culture. Maintain Hausa-native terms as canonical labels after specialist mapping; textbook and prescribed-work lists are references, not extra topic rows.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Hausa.ca90bfcfca9f88527acf.pdf) — 10 pages.

## 14. History

Nigeria before 1800: geography/peoples, early civilisations, state formation, economic development and external contacts. Nineteenth-century Nigeria: Sokoto, Borno, Yoruba states, Benin, Nupe, Igbo and Efik societies; European contact and British conquest. Colonial Nigeria: rule to 1914, amalgamation, administration, economy, social change, constitutions, nationalism and independence. Independent Nigeria: first republic, military intervention, civil war, Gowon, Murtala/Obasanjo, second republic, Buhari, Babangida, interim government, Abacha, fourth-republic transition and international membership. Wider Africa: reform/state formation in West Africa, Sierra Leone/Liberia and missions, Egypt, Ethiopia, Zulu/Mfecane and Great Trek, imperial expansion, colonial systems, decolonisation, apartheid and state-building challenges.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/History.64d0bbe89bb7585bcddc.pdf) — 12 pages.

## 15. Home-Economics

Home-economics foundations, professional areas and connections to other subjects. Household management: management principles, resources/budgeting, family relationships, marriage/reproductive health, pregnancy/birth, housing, surfaces and furnishings, sanitation and consumer awareness. Food: nutrition, meal design, cooking equipment/methods, flour products, basic mixtures, measurement/scientific techniques, kitchen planning, safety/hygiene, food storage/preservation and home gardens. Clothing: textile fibres/fabrics, sewing equipment/construction, laundering and garment care.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Home-Economics.89dc0fce33c5910e66c6.pdf) — 10 pages.

## 16. Igbo

Language: composition, comprehension, sounds, spelling, orthography, dialect/standard forms, derivation, grammar and translation. Literature: devices, oral genres, written works and prescribed readings. Culture: greetings, marriage/divorce, childbirth, titles, inheritance/funerals, kinship and community organisation, taboos, governance, hospitality, worship/objects/beliefs, land/economic organisation, associations, games, crafts and festivals. Contemporary issues in language/literature/culture; literary organisations and publications; health, substance abuse, secret groups, women/children’s rights, abuses, religious conflict and kidnapping. Preserve diacritics; confirm individual prescribed texts separately for the exam year.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Igbo.7a09449bbeefc39b4f44.pdf) — 9 pages.

## 17. Islamic-Studies

Qur’anic revelation, commentary, recitation principles, selected Arabic passages and their lessons; Hadith development/selection and moral teaching. Belief/jurisprudence: monotheism, faith articles, worship practices, family matters, legal sources/schools, economic principles and political principles. History: pre-Islamic Arabia, Muhammad’s life, early caliphs, Islam’s African contacts, West African influence and contributions to education. Selected surahs, Hadith numbers and verse references must be recorded as child identifiers during specialist review, not collapsed into a generic religion tag.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Islamic.973a07ea012af6554ed3.pdf) — 13 pages.

## 18. Literature-In-English

Drama: forms, dramatic methods and African/non-African prescribed plays. Prose: forms, narrative methods and prescribed African/non-African works. Poetry: forms, techniques and prescribed African/non-African poems. Cross-genre terminology and appreciation of unseen passages. Keep work, author, genre, region and prescribed-year range separately; the downloaded portal document’s text list is not proof that every title remains prescribed for the next examination.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Literature.f24a00818658eb8ecbd0.pdf) — 6 pages.

## 19. Mathematics

Number systems: bases/modular operations, fractions/decimals, approximation, indices, logarithms, surds and sets. Algebra: polynomials, variation, inequalities, sequences/series, binary operations, matrices and determinants. Spatial mathematics: plane geometry, measurement of shapes/solids, loci, coordinate methods and trigonometry. Calculus: differentiation, applications and integration. Statistics/combinatorics: displaying data, central measures, dispersion, arrangements/selections and probability.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Mathematics.c0621f4b8b674e75fd05.pdf) — 6 pages.

## 20. Music

Notation foundations: staff, note/rest values, time signatures, scales/keys, keyboard/enharmonic relationships, intervals, performance terms and notation conversion. Harmony: triads/inversions, chord progressions, voice motion, cadences, non-chord notes, modulation and elementary composition. African music: Nigerian folk traditions, forms, instruments, composers, performers and popular-music development. Western music: historical periods, composers, forms and performance media. Comparative study: African-American traditions, black musicians and Nigerian musical nationalism. Specific composer/work/instrument lists remain child entries requiring music-editor review.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Music.6a7142057d840d45959d.pdf) — 9 pages.

## 21. Physics

Measurement/units; scalar/vector quantities; motion; gravitation; force balance; work/energy/power; friction; machines; elasticity; pressure; fluid statics; temperature; thermal energy; phase changes; expansion; gas relationships; vapour behaviour; particle models; heat transfer; waves; sound propagation and properties; light; mirrors/reflection; refraction; optical devices; colour/dispersion; electrostatic effects; capacitance; cells; direct-current circuits; electrical energy/power; magnetism; current-force effects; induction; alternating-current circuits; conduction in different media; modern/atomic/nuclear physics; basic electronic systems; fibre-optic and laser foundations.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Physics.16527a294cb7e7a87a35.pdf) — 20 pages.

## 22. Physical and Health Education

Physical/health-education foundations and history; anatomy, body systems and posture; sport/game principles and practice; nutrition and drugs; fitness and conditioning; recreation, leisure and dance; first aid and safety; national/international sport; corrective/adapted activity; personal/community/environmental health; infectious and noninfectious disease; family life and sexuality education. Sport-specific rules/events and body-system details should be child topics in the complete mapping, retaining the source section structure.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/PHE.d3add6c626efcb3f0f2b.pdf) — 11 pages.

## 23. Principles of Accounts

Bookkeeping foundations and accounting purpose; double-entry methods; ethics and professional regulation; cash records; bank reconciliation; sole-trader final accounts; inventory valuation; control ledgers; incomplete/single-entry records; manufacturing accounts; nonprofit accounts; departmental and branch accounting; joint ventures; partnerships; company accounts; public-sector accounting; digital accounting applications.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Principles-of-Accounting.64fc902ad37449d1f6ff.pdf) — 6 pages.

## 24. Use of English

Reading and summary: passage types, central ideas, inference, author intent/tone, grammatical functions, figurative usage and cloze exercises; prescribed reading work as an exam-year-specific entity. Vocabulary/grammar: similar/opposite meanings, clause/sentence interpretation, expression meaning, word use, spelling, sentence patterns and idioms. Oral English: vowel categories, consonants/clusters, rhymes, stress and emphatic stress. Preserve source distinctions between syllabus content and examination distribution; do not invent novel titles or silently alter the app’s 60-question English requirement.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Use-of-English.885e843b1f51e6a3d062.pdf) — 5 pages.

## 25. Yoruba

Language: prose/verse comprehension, composition, sound/spelling system, tones, word formation, grammar, meaning and translation. Literature: oral narrative, poetry and performance genres; written drama, prose and poetry; prescribed works and literary appreciation. Culture: belief systems, customary institutions, social life, ceremonies, occupations, entertainment and material practices. Preserve Yoruba orthography and tone marks; exact native-language subheadings and annual prescribed works require a Yoruba content editor before activation.

Source: [official PDF](https://ibass.jamb.gov.ng/static/media/Yoruba.c9f0848e61ff86f1349b.pdf) — 5 pages.
