## Random selection and database implementation

- Store versioned blueprint cells, counts and explicit question classifications in PostgreSQL. These tables describe the domain, not hardcoded topic-name arrays in runtime JavaScript. A new topic appears in the taxonomy dynamically but requires an editorial cell assignment before mock inclusion.
- Exactly one primary cell per question per blueprint. Context-specific assessment assignment need not rewrite its practice topic or historical evidence. Shared passages/work/chapter identifiers remain explicit.
- Generate a private new seed for each newly issued form. Random rank inside eligible cells; prefer less-exposed items and spread across mapped topics/chapters. Never use upload position or consecutive database rows as the sampling rule.
- Keep comprehension/cloze items together; cloze gaps stay ordered. Shuffle standalone questions within their configured sections. Freeze selected IDs, order, editorial revision, private keys, deadline and blueprint manifest for resume.
- Require unique question IDs and normalised content families. Default difficulty30/50/20 and <=50% previous-form overlap are soft preferences. Log unavoidable relaxation; do not promise exactly50% repeat probability or force half of the items to repeat.
- Empty/short mandatory cells fail before an attempt or free diagnostic claim is committed. Do not silently borrow from another subject/section. Display the subject and missing cell so administrators can repair coverage.
