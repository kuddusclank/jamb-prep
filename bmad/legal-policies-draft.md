## 1. Privacy Policy

### 1.1 Who we are and who this policy covers

JAMBPrep is an online study service for students preparing for the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME). It is jointly operated by **Clank Dynamics Solutions and VABytes Solutions** under the LecternIQ brand. They jointly determine how and why personal information is processed through JAMBPrep and are referred to together as “JAMBPrep”, “we”, “us” or “our”. This policy explains how we handle personal information when you visit our site, create an account, study, take a mock examination, contact us or make a payment.

JAMBPrep is an independent preparation service. We are not JAMB and are not affiliated with or endorsed by JAMB.

### 1.2 Information we handle

Depending on how you use JAMBPrep, we may handle:

- **Account and identity information:** account ID, email address, display name, sign-in and session information, and account status.
- **Study profile:** exam year entered for reference, selected subjects and subject combination, target score, and onboarding answers.
- **Learning activity:** questions issued, answers and review choices, practice sessions, mock attempts, scores, timing and progress summaries used to provide results and topic-strength insights.
- **Content and support submissions:** question flags or reports, app ratings, messages and information you choose to include in a support request.
- **Referral information:** referral codes, who referred an account, and the status of referral eligibility or rewards.
- **Payment and subscription records:** transaction references, amounts, currency, payment and subscription status, access dates, cancellation information and limited tokenized payment/authorisation details needed to verify or renew a subscription. A third-party payment processor handles payment-card entry and processing; JAMBPrep does not ask you to send your card number or security code to our support team.
- **Device and technical information:** browser/device characteristics, IP address and request or security logs where made available by our hosting providers, and information needed to diagnose errors, protect accounts and prevent misuse.
- **Device storage:** session tokens and locally stored study/session data may be kept in your browser so that sign-in, offline answer syncing or session recovery can work. The data stored locally can remain on a shared device until you sign out or clear that browser’s site data.

Do not include sensitive personal information in question reports or support messages unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

### 1.3 Why we use information

We use personal information to:

1. create and secure accounts, authenticate users and provide the service they request;
2. save answers, resume eligible sessions, grade attempts and show study history and progress;
3. select practice questions and mock examinations based on chosen subjects and the available question bank;
4. administer subscriptions, confirm payments, provide paid access, handle renewals and cancellations, and detect payment errors;
5. administer referrals and display or apply rewards under the referral rules shown in the service;
6. answer support requests, review ratings and question reports, and improve the service;
7. protect JAMBPrep and its users, investigate suspected fraud or abuse, troubleshoot technical problems and enforce our terms; and
8. comply with legal, accounting and regulatory obligations and establish or defend legal claims.

We do not use your mock answers to make decisions about admission, school placement or your official JAMB result.

### 1.4 Lawful grounds

As applicable to the specific activity, we rely on one or more lawful grounds under Nigerian data-protection law: providing the service or taking steps you request before a contract; complying with legal obligations; legitimate interests such as account security, service reliability and fraud prevention where those interests do not override your rights; and consent where consent is required. Where we rely on consent, you may withdraw it, although this does not affect processing already carried out lawfully or processing that has another lawful ground.

The operators should confirm and document the lawful ground for each data flow before publication.

### 1.5 Providers and disclosures

We use external service providers for functions such as hosting and delivering the service, account authentication and data storage, payment processing, and sending transactional emails such as account-recovery messages. Each provider receives only the information needed for its function and processes it under its applicable terms and privacy commitments. Payment-card information is entered with the payment processor; we may receive transaction references, payment status and limited tokenized authorisation details needed to confirm access or administer a subscription.

We may also disclose limited information to professional advisers, auditors, regulators, law-enforcement authorities or other parties when required by law, necessary to protect users or our rights, or needed to resolve a dispute or suspected fraud. We do not sell student personal information.

Some providers may process information outside Nigeria. The operators confirm that safeguards are in place for such processing. Before publication, they must document the relevant provider locations and transfer grounds and ensure the public notice accurately describes the arrangements required by applicable law.

### 1.6 Cookies and similar storage

JAMBPrep uses browser storage and similar technologies needed for authentication, account security, saved study sessions, user preferences and service operation. Some information is stored on your device so that study work can be restored or synced after a connection interruption. Deleting browser storage can sign you out or remove unsynced local study data.

This draft does not describe optional advertising or analytics cookies because no such product use has been confirmed. If optional tracking is introduced, we will update this policy and provide any choices or notices required by law.

### 1.7 Retention and deletion

We keep information for as long as needed for the purposes described above, including while your account is active, to provide study history, maintain payment and subscription records, protect exam integrity, resolve support issues and meet legal obligations. When information is no longer needed, we will delete it or remove identifying details, subject to lawful retention, backup and dispute-resolution needs.

You may request account closure or deletion using the contact details below. We will explain if particular records must be retained and why. Deleting browser storage on your device is separate from requesting deletion of information held by JAMBPrep. The operators have confirmed that retention and deletion arrangements are in place; records are kept only for the periods needed for the stated purposes and applicable legal obligations.

**Operators to complete before publication:** state the operational retention periods for inactive accounts, mock/answer records, payment records, security logs and backups, and the account deletion workflow.

### 1.8 Security

We use technical and organisational safeguards appropriate to the information and service, including access controls and provider security features. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. Please use a unique password, protect access to your email and device, sign out on shared devices, and contact us promptly if you suspect account misuse.

### 1.9 Your rights and choices

Subject to applicable law and the circumstances of a request, you may ask us to provide access to your information, correct inaccurate information, erase or restrict certain processing, object to processing, receive portable information, withdraw consent, or obtain information about our processing. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission (NDPC).

To exercise a privacy right, email **contact@clankdynamics.com** from the address associated with your account and describe your request. You may address the request to either joint controller through this shared contact. We may need to verify your identity and may ask for clarification. We will respond within the time required by applicable law. You may also contact the NDPC through its official website: <https://ndpc.gov.ng/>.

### 1.10 Children and young people

JAMB candidates may be under 18. If you are under 18, a parent or legal guardian must be involved and provide consent before you create an account or use paid features. A parent or guardian may contact us at **contact@clankdynamics.com** with questions or a request concerning a child’s information. We may take reasonable steps to verify the request and the parent or guardian’s authority.

**Before publication:** ensure the live registration and account flows actually obtain and record any consent or age verification required by applicable law; this policy statement alone does not create that product process.

You retain rights in material you submit, while allowing us to use it as needed to operate the service, respond to you, moderate reports and improve the service. Practice and mock sessions may save answers locally and sync them to our services. You are responsible for keeping a reliable connection when required and checking whether answers have synced before closing a session. We will make reasonable efforts to recover sessions, but an interruption, device failure or unsynced local answer may prevent recovery.

Where an exam-conditions mode is offered, the interface may record events such as leaving or hiding the exam page as interruption events. Such events are indicators only and do not by themselves establish misconduct. JAMBPrep does not use a microphone or camera for this feature. If you cannot cancel in-app, contact **contact@clankdynamics.com** before the next renewal and include your account email and subscription reference.

## 3. Refund Policy

### 3.1 How to request a refund

**All refund requests must state the reason and be emailed to contact@clankdynamics.com.** Include:

- the email address on your JAMBPrep account;
- the payment transaction reference;
- the amount and approximate payment date;
- the reason for the request and a short description of what happened; and
- the outcome you are asking us to consider (for example, restoring access, correcting a billing issue, or refunding an amount).

Please do not email card numbers, PINs, passwords, one-time passcodes or CVV/security codes. We may ask for non-sensitive information needed to locate and verify the transaction.

### 3.2 How we assess requests

We review refund requests individually and consider the reason, whether payment was authorised and correctly recorded, whether the paid service was made available and performed as described, how much of the paid period or service was available or used, any technical failure, and applicable law. Depending on the circumstances, we may restore access, correct an account or billing issue, provide another appropriate remedy, or approve a full or reasonable partial refund.

An accidental duplicate payment, an unauthorised transaction, a payment recorded without the promised access, or a material service failure should be reported promptly with the transaction reference so we can investigate. A payment-provider status may take time to reconcile; we will not treat a payment as confirmed solely from a browser redirect or screenshot.

### 3.3 Renewals and cancellation

Turning off auto-renewal prevents future renewal charges but does not itself refund a charge already made. Paid access normally continues until the end of the paid period. If you believe a renewal was charged after cancellation or was otherwise incorrect, email **contact@clankdynamics.com** with the account email, cancellation date if known, and transaction reference.

### 3.4 No blanket waiver of legal rights

This policy does not promise that every change-of-mind request will be refunded. However, it does not remove rights or remedies available under applicable law. In particular, where a service has not been performed to the required standard, Nigerian consumer law may entitle a consumer to require a remedy or a reasonable portion of the price back, having regard to the extent of the failure. We will assess such requests on their facts and will not rely on this policy to waive a non-waivable right.
