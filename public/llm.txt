## UTME Structure & Canonical Admissions Rules

- **Total Examination Mark**: 400 marks across 4 subjects.
- **Compulsory Subject**: Use of English is strictly compulsory for 100% of candidates regardless of faculty or course (60 questions).
- **Course Electives**: Candidates select exactly 3 course-specific electives (40 questions each) matching the official JAMB brochure requirements.
- **Flexible Electives**: Certain disciplines allow candidates to select from approved alternatives (e.g., Computer Science candidates choosing between Chemistry or Biology, Agriculture candidates choosing Biology or Agricultural Science).
- **O'Level Requirements**: Most competitive courses (e.g., Medicine, Pharmacy, Law, Nursing, Engineering) strictly require at least 5 credit passes (including English Language and Mathematics) at a single sitting in WAEC, NECO, or NABTEB.

## High-Volume Course Combinations

- **Medicine and Surgery (MBBS)**: Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics. Recommended competitive target: 300+.
- **Computer Science**: Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry (or Biology/Computer Studies). Recommended competitive target: 260+.
- **Common Law / Civil Law (LL.B)**: Use of English, Literature in English, and any two Arts/Social Science subjects (Government, CRS/IRS, Economics, History). Literature in English is strictly compulsory. Recommended competitive target: 270+.
- **Nursing Science**: Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics. Recommended competitive target: 270+.
- **Accounting / Accountancy**: Use of English, Mathematics, Economics, and one other Commercial/Social Science subject (Financial Accounting, Commerce, Government). Recommended competitive target: 250+.
- **Pharmacy (Pharm.D)**: Use of English, Biology, Chemistry, Physics. Recommended competitive target: 280+.
- **Mechanical / Electrical / Civil Engineering**: Use of English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry. Recommended competitive target: 260+.
- **Mass Communication**: Use of English, Literature in English, Economics, Government. Recommended competitive target: 250+.
